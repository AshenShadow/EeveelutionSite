Eeveelutions

Eevee

Eevee is the base of the eeveelutions, being able to evolve into any of the 17. It can be evolved with a stone or by meeting conditions.

Eevee is a fox-like creature with thick, soft, white fur around its neck and the end of its tail. Compared to the others, it's the smallest at an average height of 0.3m (1'00") and an average weight of 6.5kg (14.3 lbs).

Eevee's the most common of the eeveelutions, most commonly found in large plains or light forests, living among the roots of trees in dens.

Strike Eevee

A special form of Eevee. Rarely found in storms or the highest mountains, this black-furred variant has a very high price on the market.

Its most notable features are its light blue eyes and the light blue markings on its body.

!!! Strike Eevee is heavily hunted for its unique fur markings. If you find one, please make sure to report it to the city's pokemon safety center. !!!

Spirit Eevee

A special form of Eevee. Found amongst the graves and tombstones of graveyards, It's nearly always suspected to be the lingering spirit of a dead pokemon.

This form's recognizable due to its fur and fluff being the same color.

Vaporeon

Vaporeon is the water type evolution. This evolution is guaranteed upon an Eevee coming in contact with a Water Stone

Instead of fur, most Vaporeons have hard scales covering their body. Its most recognizable features are its long mermaid-like tail, white neck ruff and three fin-like ears. It has an average height of 1m (3'03") and an average weight of 29kg (63.9 lbs).

Vaporeon's found around wet areas. It's most commonly seen around rivers, ponds, lakes and oceans, but some reports claim rare sightings in swamps.

Strike Vaporeon

A special form of Vaporeon. Rarely found in storms and high mountains around large bodies of water, this black-scaled variant has a very high price on the market.

It's most notable features are its light blue eyes and the light blue markings on its body.

!!! Strike Vaporeon is heavily hunted for its unique scale markings. If you find one, please make sure to report it to the city's pokemon safety center. !!!

Spirit Vaporeon

A special form of Vaporeon. Found rarely amongst the graves and tombstones of graveyards, It's nearly always suspected to be the lingering spirit of a dead pokemon.

This form's recognizable due to it'S scales and fins being the same color.

Flareon

Flareon is the fire type evolution. This evolution is guaranteed upon an Eevee coming in contact with a Fire Stone

Flareon has the most fluff out of all the eeveelutions. These fluffs are it's most recognizable features as well, having the biggest tail and neck fluff of all the eeveelutions. It has an average height of 0.9 m (2'11") and an average weight of 25 kg (55.1 lbs)

Flareon's found around the hottest environments. Most common sightings include deserts and bases of volcanoes, but witnesses have also claimed to rarely see it in Savannas and around natural hot springs.

Blue Moon Flareon

A special form of Flareon. Possibly one of the rarest special forms because it's only found during a celestial event called a blue moon.

Blue Moon Flareon has a unique evolution condition: An Eevee must come in contact with a Fire Stone and must be exposed to the reflected deep blue light of a Blue Moon.

Blue Moon Flareon is identical to a regular Flareon, except it has black fur and dark blue fluff. This blue fluff, suspected to be made of a special material, Blue Moon Flareon is amongst the most valued pokemon on the market.

!!! Blue Moon Flareon is heavily hunted for its fur and price. If you ever encounter one please make sure to report it to the city's pokemon safety center. !!!

A special form of Flareon. Found rarely amongst the graves and tombstones of graveyards, It's nearly always suspected to be the lingering spirit of a dead pokemon.

Leafeon

Leafeon is the grass type evolution. This evolution is guaranteed upon an Eevee coming in contact with a Leaf Stone or having Eevee level up near a giant moss stone.

Leafeon's most recognizable features are its forehead leaf and its leaf-like ears and tail. It also has several smaller leaf-like structures attached to its body. It has an average height of 1 m (3'03") and an average weight of 25.5 kg (56.2 lbs)

Leafeon is mostly found in dense forests and overgrown caves, but it's rarely been spotted wandering in swamps or flower fields.

Seasonal Leafeons

Special forms of Leafeon. Seasonal Leafeons are extremely rare. They're Leafeons who have adapted to the harsh conditions of different environments.

Spring Leafeon has slightly brighter fur and pink leaves, Summer Leafeon has darker fur and brighter leaves, Autumn Leafeon has slightly darker fur and orange leaves, and Winter Leafeon has near-white fur and pine green leaves.

Believed to be protectors of the seasons, hunters are skeptical in hunting them.

Galacteon

Galacteon is a cosmic type pokemon. Not much is known about them. There have only been 3 real encounters recorded. Attempts to capture and study them have been heavily unsuccessful.

It's highly theorized that Galacteons are protectors of space. While their evolution line is unknown, their Eevee like appearance suggests they could be an eeveelution, though it's not confirmed.

Galacteons are extremely rarely found roaming around anywhere on the planet. A few space missions revealed that there's more of them out there, as there have been a total of 7 sightings of Galacteons floating through space.
