Eeveelutions

Eevee

Eevee is the source of all the eeveelutions. Being the "baby version" of each of them, it's likely you'll see eevees in other eeveelutions' nests.

As innocent as it looks, Eevee can pack quite a punch when used for offense. However it's nearly equal defense stats mean it can also be used as quite the defender if needed.

Eevee's most recognizable feature is most likely it's thick, white fur around it's neck. It can most commonly be found around plains and forests.

Strike Eevee

Strike Eevee is a black Eevee with blue eyes and markings all over it's body and tail. It's most active during storms, often resting in abandoned places.

Spirit Eevee

Spirit Eevee is a pure white Eevee. Being slightly lighter and similar than shiny, it's often mistaken for it. It's most commonly found around graveyards.

!!! Please note that the special forms above are always hunted for their price. They're worth significantly more than shinies. !!!

Vaporeon

Vaporeon is one of the original, generation I eeveelutions. It has the most common evolution rate at 34% out of the 3.

Its fish-like fins and dolphin-like tail allow it to swim extremely quickly underwater.

Strike Vaporeon

Strike Vaporeon is a black Vaporeon with light blue eyes and markings on it's body. It's most active during storms.

Spirit Vaporeon

Spirit Vaporeon is a pure white Vaporeon. It has slightly less weight due to it's spirit-like build.

!!! Special forms are high-value targets. Proceed with caution. !!!

Flareon

Flareon is the second eeveelution out of the original trio. Preferred by families for its warmth.

Flareon is a great defender due to its fluff, which can absorb attacks.

Spirit Flareon

Spirit Flareon is a black Flareon. It's slightly lighter due to its spirit-like build.

!!! High Value Target !!!

Jolteon

Jolteon is the third eeveelution of the trio. Often used for charging electrical devices.

It is the fastest eeveelution on the ground.

Strike Jolteon

Strike Jolteon is a black Jolteon with light blue markings. It has increased lightning abilities.

!!! High Value Target !!!

Umbreon

Umbreon is the first of the second generation. It hides easily in the dark.

Strike Umbreon

Strike Umbreon is a black Umbreon with light blue rings.

Ashen Umbreon

Body made of ash and luminescent bodies. Extremely dangerous.

!!! High Value Target !!!

Espeon

Espeon is the psychic eeveelution. It can manipulate minds and objects.

Strike Espeon

Strike Espeon is a black Espeon with light blue markings.

!!! High Value Target !!!

Leafeon

Leafeon is the ally of gardeners. It can manipulate plants.

Seasonal Leafeons

Spring (Pink flowers), Summer (Green), Autumn (Brown/Jade), Winter (Ice/Blue).

!!! High Value Target !!!

Glaceon

Glaceon can create any form of ice, limited only by its imagination.

Spirit Glaceon

Black Glaceon with purple-pink ice features.

!!! High Value Target !!!

Sylveon

Sylveon uses its ribbons to lift heavy objects, though limited by its own strength.

Spirit Sylveon

Black Sylveon with purple-pink ribbons.

!!! High Value Target !!!
