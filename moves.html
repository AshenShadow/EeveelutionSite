Custom Moves

These moves are unique to our ecosystem. They follow standard mechanics but offer new strategic depth.

Leaf Sprint

Status PP: 15 Pwr: - Acc: -
Allows the pokemon to enter a pile of leaves, and exit out another one. Most effective during Autumn. Takes 2 turns.

Lightweight

Status PP: 30 Pwr: - Acc: -
Increases the user's evasion and speed stats.

Ultra Hydropump

Special PP: 5 Pwr: 300% Acc: ∞
Takes 2 turns. Releases a blast with infinite accuracy. Afterwards, user's stats harshly decrease and takes 25 recoil damage. Cooldown: 3 turns.

Core Collapse

Special PP: 1 Pwr: ∞ Acc: ∞
Turn 9+ Only. This move eliminates everything in existence, including pokemon in pokeballs. Exclusive to Cosmic types.

Star Shooter

Special PP: 10 Pwr: 25 Acc: 85%
The user summons shooting stars to hit the opponent. Hits multiple times. The move ends when it misses.

Cosmic Fang

Physical PP: 20 Pwr: 75 Acc: 100%
The user bites the opponent with fangs powered by outer space.

Supernova

Special PP: 5 Pwr: 150 Acc: 80%
Takes 2 turns. Turn 1: Summons a star. Turn 2: Detonates the star for a devastating blow.

Asteroid Rain

Status PP: 15 Pwr: - Acc: -
Lasts 5 turns. Summons an asteroid rain. Each turn damages both sides for 5% of their max HP.
