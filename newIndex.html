About our community

We're a very small, and ever-so-slowly growing community made of most eeveelutions. A DM group that started as a group of 4 turned into this server with over 30 members (some active, some not).

We're very happy to have founded this community, and we hope that you can join in too! More members are always welcome.

While most people would only know the original 9 eeveelutions, we have custom ones too! We have an eeveelution for each type, as well as an entirely custom type with it's respective eeveelution. These eeveelutions can be viewed on the "Eeveelutions" page.

Our server's history

We went through a very long 1 year long journey to get to here. We had many struggles and obstacles through the way. If you want to learn our history, here it is!

The entire concept started as a DM group of 3. During a school day, another person saw his groupmate chat in the dm group and asked to join, since he was a big fan of eeveelutions. After he joined, more and more people started to appear overtime. So many people, in face, that the dm group was almost full!

A while later before the group was full, we decided to create a server to hopefully accept more people. And we continued growing, and growing. Unfortunately after a few months of growing decently positive, a huge wave of negativity hit and we lost a few members. Right after this negative wave, a very small positive wave followed, after which a member decided it was fun to completely destroy a person from the community. Since then we lost many more members due to ensuring the safety of the server. After we got rid of one person like that, who could've single-handedly ruined the entire community and left it in dust, he decided to start spreading misinformation and lies about us. Since then we've been at war with them.

And now we arrive to today. Our community is doing very nice right now, and we're not in any immediate danger. But I'm sure the clouds will set upon us and we'll get a negative wave again.
