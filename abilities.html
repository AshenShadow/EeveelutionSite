Custom Abilities

These abilities are unique to our ecosystem.

Abilities are special traits acquired on birth or through evolution.

Formswitch

Allows the pokemon to switch forms at will. There are currently 2 forms registered.

Form: Berserk

Calling: Transform

• All stats +50%
• Move Power +50%
• Max HP +200%

Form: Ultra

Calling: Ultrify

• All stats +200%
• Move Power +300%
• Max HP +1100%

Shellshift

Allows the pokemon to devolve for 30 minutes. While devolved, the pokemon is unable to evolve into anything else.

My Creations

The pokemon heals 17% hp each turn.

If the pokemon is the last of its team, Attack is increased by 500% for 5 turns and revives all fallen allies to 50% health. (No effect on "Core Collapse")
